Find the weekdays and scheduled departure hours associated with higher arrival-delay rates. Each cell pools eligible arrivals across the selected months, airline, and airport.

Gray = no observations (never a zero rate). Gray-blue = scheduled flights with no eligible arrivals. Dashed outline = low sample (fewer than 30 eligible arrivals). Observed zero delay renders at the 0% end of the color scale.
How to read this view
  • Rows run Monday through Sunday; columns use scheduled local departure hour, 0–23. The BTS 2400 convention maps to 00; this is not a UTC comparison.
  • Each rate is arrivals at least 15 minutes late / noncancelled, nondiverted arrivals with observed arrival delay. Cells pool counts before calculating the rate.
  • Hover a cell for scheduled flights, eligible arrivals, delayed arrivals, and rate. Treat cells with fewer than 30 eligible arrivals cautiously; no observations and an observed 0% rate have different meanings.

Reported Cause Comparison: 100% Stacked Bars

Compare which reported categories account for delay minutes across airports or airlines. Each bar shows the distribution of attributed minutes within a group, helping you spot differences in composition.

How to read this view
  • Each segment is a category's observed attributed minutes / total reported attributed minutes for that group. A 100% bar shows composition, not the number of flights or total delay burden.
  • Airport mode compares the busiest 12 origin airports under the month and airline filters, adding the selected airport when needed. Airline mode compares all reporting airlines under the selected months and airport.
  • Hover a segment or row label for minutes, shares, and attribution observation/completeness counts. Missing attribution is not zero; zero-total and all-missing groups do not form complete bars.
  • The categories describe reported attribution. WeatherDelay excludes weather effects attributed to other categories, including NAS; the bars do not establish causal effects.

Bars with a positive reported-minute total sum to 100%. Groups whose attribution is missing or totals zero are listed with an explicit note — never drawn as complete bars. Missing attribution is not zero; Weather excludes weather attributed to NAS. Descriptive, not causal.

City-level Flight Network Overview

Locate major departure hubs and busy city connections, then choose an airport to explore its outgoing routes. Circle area shows annual scheduled departures; circle color shows the arrival-delay rate of outgoing flights.

How to read this view
  • The overview pools airports into cities. Curved lines show up to 220 busiest projectable city-pairs, combining both directions; line width represents flight volume. Within-city flights are not drawn as city-pair edges.
  • Click a city to choose an airport; cities with several airports ask which one you mean. A selected airport adds its busiest outgoing directed routes at full-year, all-airline scope. Filtered statistics are in Airport Detail.
  • The map stays at full-year 2025 scope. It uses departure-weighted airport coordinates and excludes nonprojectable locations only from drawing, not from national totals. All airports remain reachable through the airport selector.
  • Delay rate uses eligible arrivals. Color is clipped to a documented percentile range, so similar colors do not guarantee identical rates; hover a city for its value and member airports.

City locations are departure-weighted airport coordinates, not city centers. Territories outside the Albers USA projection remain in national totals. Color is clipped to the 5th–95th percentile range among cities with at least 10,000 departures; all projectable cities are drawn. Gray means no eligible arrivals.

Fixed scope: this city-level overview always shows the full-year 2025, all reporting airlines, all origin airports, so it does not respond to the month/airline filters. The selected airport is highlighted with its busiest outgoing routes at that same fixed scope; filtered statistics live in Airport Detail.
