# Week 3 data preparation

**Goal:** Execute proposal.md Week 3: cleaned 2025 flight data, coordinate validation, quality report and exploratory summaries.

**Approach:** Preserve monthly BTS ZIP files with SHA-256 provenance. Process one month at a time with Python/pandas and write Parquet. Export count-based CSV summaries for D3. Keep missing causes distinct from observed zero and use the proposal's outcome denominators.

- Download and validate all 12 official monthly archives using scripts/download_data.py; record URLs, byte sizes, ZIP members and checksums.
- Acquire the BTS Master Coordinate table; require unique AirportSeqID and audit both origin and destination joins without inflating records.
- Inspect actual schema before selecting columns. Derive month, weekday, local departure hour and directed route; retain cancellations/diversions and audit duplicates rather than silently discarding ambiguous flights.
- Write scripts/prepare_data.py to produce monthly Parquet, airport metadata, quality JSON/Markdown and dimension-preserving CSV summaries.
- Validate edge cases (15-minute threshold, cancelled/diverted denominator exclusions, missing arrival delay, midnight, missing causes), reconcile summaries to monthly counts, and independently recompute representative totals.
- Document commands, fields, source links, sizes and any remaining limitations in data/README.md. Preserve proposal and unrelated files.
